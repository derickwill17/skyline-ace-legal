SKYLINE ACE Privacy Policy

SKYLINE ACE can be played offline without an account. The game does not sell or share personal information and does not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, email addresses, or raw gameplay inputs. Tap, click, and Space actions are never logged or transmitted.

Ranked Flight is optional. If you never open it, nothing about you leaves your device unless you switch on anonymous flight data yourself (see below).

Progress stored on your device

Scores, settings, unlocked content, Silver Coins, your callsign, your flight history, and career progress are stored locally on your device. On iOS and Android this uses the operating system's application preferences. In the web version it uses browser storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may erase this progress. The game keeps no server copy of your local career.

Ranked Flight (optional)

Creating a pilot in Ranked Flight creates an anonymous online account. The game does not ask for an email address or a password. Your pilot code is assigned by the server and cannot be chosen or edited. You may optionally choose a pilot name that is shown beside your code on the leaderboards; it is checked against a word filter before it is saved, other pilots can report it, and the operator can reset it to your pilot code. Do not use your real name. Because there is no email on the account, the pilot code is the only thing that identifies it.

When you fly ranked, the following is stored on our servers:

  • an account identifier and the pilot code assigned to it;
  • the pilot name you chose, if any, when you last changed it, and any reports you sent or received about a pilot name;
  • the region you selected when you created the pilot;
  • the results of each completed ranked flight: score, gates, distance, near-misses, combo and streak figures, bonus points, and flight duration;
  • your daily and season placements, and the ranked tickets used each day;
  • your Gold balance, its ledger, what you spent Gold on in the ranked store, and the store purchase receipts described under Purchases;
  • the identifiers of the airframe and emblem you have equipped, so they can be shown beside your leaderboard row.

This information is used to run the leaderboards, to credit and spend Gold, and to prevent cheating: every ranked score is recalculated by the server from these figures, and the server enforces the daily ticket allowance. It is held on servers operated by our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. It is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone else.

To keep you signed in, the app stores a session credential for the account on your device. On iOS and Android it is kept in the operating system's keychain or keystore. In the web version it is kept in browser storage. Signing out or deleting the pilot removes it.

Purchases

Gold packs are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us: the store handles payment under its own privacy policy. To credit Gold, we receive and keep the store's transaction identifier, the product bought, and the store's transaction record, together with the amount of Gold credited. If the store grants a refund, the Gold from that purchase is removed from your balance; a balance is never taken below zero. Refunds themselves are handled by Apple or Google, not by the publisher.

Anonymous flight data (opt-in)

Flight Settings has a Share anonymous flight data switch. It is off by default. When you turn it on, the app sends coarse counts: the kind of event (a session started, a run ended, Ranked opened, a ranked flight submitted, a leaderboard viewed, settings opened), a band of gates cleared rather than an exact number, the event's order within that app session, the game version, the platform, whether the screen is phone- or desktop-sized, and the hour of the day. These rows carry no account identifier, pilot code, device identifier, or free text, are never linked to a pilot, and are only ever read as totals. Turn the switch off at any time to stop sending them.

Deleting your pilot

On the Ranked Flight screen, choose Delete online account, read the Delete this pilot? notice, and confirm with Delete pilot. The account is deleted immediately, and with it your pilot code, your ranked flights and scores, your daily and season placements, your ticket history, your Gold balance and ledger, your ranked-store purchases, your equipped-item identifiers, and the purchase receipts held for the account — your chosen pilot name and the name reports you sent go with it. Any remaining Gold is forfeited and cannot be restored.

Your local career, Silver, and cosmetics stay on this device. Apple's and Google's own records of your purchases are kept by the store under its policies and are not ours to delete. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your pilot by email.

Retention

Local progress remains until you reset the game, clear application data, or uninstall it. Ranked account data remains until you delete the pilot; deletion removes it at once. The store's transaction records we hold are deleted with the account. Anonymous flight-data rows are kept only as counts and are never tied back to anyone.

Your choices

You can play without ever creating a pilot. You can sign out of, or delete, a ranked pilot from the Ranked Flight screen at any time. You can switch anonymous flight data on or off in Flight Settings. You can reset locally stored progress from Flight Settings, and you can disable sound, haptics, motion, particles, and visual effects inside the app.
