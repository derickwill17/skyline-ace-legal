Official support for SKYLINE ACE

Flight support

SKYLINE ACE Release 1 is a free, offline-first game. It does not require a SKYLINE ACE account, subscription, purchase, or internet connection for Career play.

Contact

Email skylineace2026@gmail.com. SKYLINE ACE is published by Derick Will Bencion under the developer name DB DEV. The publisher is an individual developer in the United States.

For technical help, include your device model, operating-system version, game version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, identification documents, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Local progress

Career progress and settings are stored on your device. Resetting the game, clearing its application data, or uninstalling it may permanently erase that local progress. A ranked pilot holds no copy of your career, so deleted local progress cannot be recovered from it.

Ranked account and Gold

Ranked Flight uses an anonymous pilot with no email or password; your pilot code is the only handle we have, so include it in any question about a ranked account. You can delete the pilot from the Ranked Flight screen (Delete online account, then Delete pilot), or read how to delete it by email. Gold refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not by the publisher; a refund granted by the store is reflected in your Gold balance.

Pilot name rules

A pilot name is optional. It is 3–16 letters, digits, spaces, dots, dashes or underscores, shown in capitals beside your pilot code as NAME #TAG on the leaderboards, where every pilot can see it. You can change it once every 14 days; the first name is free. By saving a name you accept these rules.

A name is not allowed if it contains or amounts to:

  • profanity or slurs;
  • hate, sexual content, harassment or threats;
  • impersonation of another pilot, of the game or of its operator;
  • personal information — yours or anyone else's;
  • anything shaped like a pilot code;
  • your real name — never use it.

To report a name, choose REPORT A NAME under the STANDINGS board, pick the pilot from the rows shown and one reason: Offensive or hateful, Impersonation or Something else. From then on the reported pilot shows as their code for you. Nothing else about their game changes.

The operator reviews reports within a few days. A name that breaks these rules is reset to the pilot code and cannot be changed for 30 days. Repeated abuse may lead to the account being removed. A pilot name cannot be bought or sold, and nothing about it is for sale: no name, no colour, no badge and no priority, and Gold has no effect on it.

A region change you request applies when the season closes, not at once; finish any held flight before then. To appeal a reset or a report, email the address above with your pilot code.
