Official support for SKYLINE ACE

Flight support

SKYLINE ACE Release 1 is a free, offline-first game. It does not require a SKYLINE ACE account, subscription, purchase, or internet connection for Career play.

Contact

Email skylineace2026@gmail.com. SKYLINE ACE is published by Derick Will Bencion under the developer name DB DEV. The publisher is an individual developer in the United States.

For technical help, include your device model, operating-system version, game version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, identification documents, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Local progress

Career progress and settings are stored on your device. Resetting the game, clearing its application data, or uninstalling it may permanently erase that local progress. A ranked pilot holds no copy of your career, so deleted local progress cannot be recovered from it.

Ranked account and Gold

Ranked Flight uses an anonymous pilot with no email or password; your pilot code is the only handle we have, so include it in any question about a ranked account. You can delete the pilot from the Ranked Flight screen (Delete online account, then Delete pilot), or read how to delete it by email. Gold refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not by the publisher; a refund granted by the store is reflected in your Gold balance.
