SKYLINE ACE account deletion

Delete your SKYLINE ACE pilot

A pilot is the anonymous online account behind Ranked Flight. It has no email address and no password; a pilot name is optional and not unique, so the pilot code assigned by the server is the only thing that identifies it. You can delete it yourself from inside the game in three taps, or ask the publisher to delete it if you no longer have the app.

Delete from inside the game

  1. Open Ranked Flight from the home screen.
  2. Choose Delete online account.
  3. Read the Delete this pilot? notice and confirm with Delete pilot.

The account is deleted immediately. The app also removes the sign-in credential it kept on your device, so nothing about the pilot is left in the game.

What is deleted

Everything the account holds on our servers goes with it, at once:

  • the account identifier and the pilot code;
  • your chosen pilot name, if any, and any pilot-name reports you sent;
  • your selected region and the identifiers of the airframe and emblem you had equipped;
  • every ranked flight and score, and your place on every daily and season board;
  • your ranked ticket history;
  • your Gold balance, its ledger, and what you bought in the ranked store;
  • the store purchase receipts held for the account.

What is not deleted

  • Your local career, Silver, cosmetics, settings, and flight history stay on your device. Deleting the pilot does not touch them.
  • Apple's and Google's own records of your purchases are kept by the store under its policies. They are not ours, and we cannot delete them.

Gold is forfeited

Any Gold left on the account is lost when the pilot is deleted and cannot be restored or moved to a new pilot. Refunds for Gold packs are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not by the publisher; deleting the pilot does not request one.

If you no longer have the app

Email skylineace2026@gmail.com from any address with your pilot code. There is no email on the account, so the pilot code is the only way we can find it. A pilot name is not unique, so a name alone is not enough to find an account. The publisher completes the deletion as soon as possible and replies when it is done. Do not send passwords, identification documents, or payment information; they are not needed and we do not want them.
