SKYLINE ACE account deletion

Delete your SKYLINE ACE pilot

A pilot is the online account behind Ranked Flight. A guest pilot has no email address and no password; a saved pilot has the email, Apple ID or Google account you linked.

The only thing that identifies a pilot is the pilot code assigned by the server; a pilot name is optional and can change, be reset or be given up. You can delete the pilot yourself from inside the game in three taps, or ask the publisher to delete it if you no longer have the app.

A saved pilot can be deleted from inside the game after signing in on any device with the same email, Apple ID or Google account; a guest pilot only from the device it was created on.

Delete from inside the game

  1. Open Ranked Flight from the home screen.
  2. Choose Delete online account.
  3. Read the Delete this pilot? notice and confirm with Delete pilot.

The account is deleted immediately. If the pilot was saved with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more so we can also ask Apple to revoke its authorisation for this app; if that request fails, the account is still deleted. The app also removes the sign-in credential it kept on your device, so nothing about the pilot is left in the game.

What is deleted

Deleting removes the account, its sign-in identities and Apple's authorisation for this app. A pilot saved with Google needs no extra step: the identifier of your Google account, the email address, and the name and picture link Google sent go with the account, and we never had any other access to your Google account. You can also remove SKYLINE ACE from the apps connected to your Google account. Everything the account holds on our servers goes with it, at once:

  • the account identifier and the pilot code;
  • for a saved pilot, the email address you verified, the identifier Apple issued for this app or the identifier of your Google account with the name and picture link on it;
  • your chosen pilot name, if any, and any pilot-name reports you sent;
  • your selected region and the identifiers of the airframe and emblem you had equipped;
  • every ranked flight and score, and your place on every daily and season board;
  • your ranked ticket history;
  • your Gold balance, its ledger, and what you bought in the ranked store;
  • the store purchase receipts held for the account.

What is not deleted

  • Your local career, Silver, cosmetics, settings, and flight history stay on your device. Deleting the pilot does not touch them.
  • Apple's and Google's own records of your purchases are kept by the store under its policies. They are not ours, and we cannot delete them.

Gold is forfeited

Any Gold left on the account is lost when the pilot is deleted and cannot be restored or moved to a new pilot. Refunds for Gold packs are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not by the publisher; deleting the pilot does not request one.

If you no longer have the app

Email skylineace.help@outlook.com from any address with your pilot code. A guest pilot has no email, so the pilot code is the only way we can find it; for a saved pilot the code is still the fastest way, and writing from the address you saved it with helps us confirm it is yours.

A pilot name can change, be reset or be given up, so a name alone is not enough to find an account. The publisher completes the deletion as soon as possible and replies when it is done. Do not send passwords, identification documents, or payment information; they are not needed and we do not want them.
